# Privacy Policy

Thank you for using the Kanka app. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

## 1. Information Collected
We only collect the minimum amount of personal data necessary for the app to function:
- **Usage Data:** Your in-app interactions (page views, clicks, etc.) are collected anonymously.
- **Device Information:** Technical information such as device model and OS version is collected for debugging and performance improvements.

## 2. Use of Data
The collected data is used exclusively for:
- Improving app performance.
- Detecting and resolving bugs.
- Understanding user preferences when developing new features.

## 3. Data Security
We use modern encryption methods to keep your data secure. We never share your data with third parties for advertising purposes.

## 4. Contact
If you have any questions about our privacy policy, please contact us.
